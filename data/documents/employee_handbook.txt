
VELTRIONYX - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

1. Working Hours
Employees must work Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

2. Attendance
Employees should check in before 9:00 AM. Arrival after 9:15 AM is considered late.

3. Professional Conduct
Employees must maintain professional behavior and respect colleagues.

4. Communication
Employees should use official company communication channels for work-related discussions.

5. Employee Responsibilities
Employees must follow company policies, complete assigned tasks, and protect company information.
