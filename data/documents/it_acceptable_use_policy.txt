
VELTRIONYX - IT ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

1. Approved Software
Employees may install and use only company-approved software.

2. Internet Usage
Company internet access should primarily be used for work-related activities.

3. Device Security
Employees must keep company devices secure and updated.

4. Unauthorized Applications
Employees must not install unauthorized applications or software.

5. Reporting
IT-related issues and suspicious activities must be reported to the IT department.
