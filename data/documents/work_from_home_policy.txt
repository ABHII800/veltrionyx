
VELTRIONYX - WORK FROM HOME POLICY

1. Eligibility
Employees may work from home up to two days per week.

2. Approval
Manager approval is required before working remotely.

3. Availability
Employees must remain available during regular working hours, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

4. Productivity
Employees must complete assigned tasks and attend scheduled meetings.

5. Security
Employees must use approved devices and secure internet connections while working remotely.
