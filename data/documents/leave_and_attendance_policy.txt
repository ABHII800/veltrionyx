
VELTRIONYX - LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE POLICY

1. Casual Leave
Employees receive 8 casual leave days per year.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 8 sick leave days per year.

3. Earned Leave
Employees receive 15 earned leave days per year.

4. Personal Leave
Personal leave is subject to manager approval.

5. Late Arrival
Arrival after 9:15 AM is considered late.

6. Leave Requests
Employees must submit leave requests through the approved process.

7. Attendance
Employees must record their daily check-in and check-out times.
