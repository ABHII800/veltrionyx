
VELTRIONYX - INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

1. Data Protection
Employees must protect confidential company and customer information.

2. Password Security
Employees must use strong passwords and must never share their credentials.

3. Security Incidents
Employees must immediately report security incidents to the IT department.

4. Access Control
Employees should access only the information required for their job responsibilities.

5. Data Sharing
Confidential company information must not be shared with unauthorized individuals.
